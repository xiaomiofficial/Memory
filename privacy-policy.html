Memory: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Summary

Memory is a photo and video gallery. Your photos stay on your phone. Memory has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we never receive your photos, videos, location or albums.

This page explains what the app does with your data, the few times it uses the internet, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Memory is developed by the developer behind github.com/xiaomiofficial/Memory. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/xiaomiofficial/Memory/issues.

What Memory accesses on your phone

Photos and videos: with your permission, Memory reads the photos and videos in your library so it can show them, organise them and edit them. Editing always saves a new copy; your originals are not changed unless you ask (for example when you adjust a photo's date or location, or delete it).

Photo location: with the 'access media location' permission, Memory reads the GPS position stored inside your photos, only to group photos by place and show them on your device.

Fingerprint / face / screen lock: used only through Android's own prompt to protect the Hidden album and App Lock. Memory never receives or stores your fingerprint, face or PIN.

What is stored, and where

Memory keeps a small database in its private app storage: analysis results (for example face counts, scene labels, place names and image fingerprints used to find duplicates), your albums, favorites, recent searches, settings and video playback positions.

Hidden photos are moved into Memory's private storage. They are hidden from other apps by the Android app sandbox and need your fingerprint, face or screen lock to open inside Memory. They are not additionally encrypted.

All of this stays on your device. It is deleted if you clear Memory's storage or uninstall the app. Uninstalling also deletes photos that are in the Hidden album, so unhide anything you want to keep first.

On-device analysis

Faces, scenes, objects, duplicates and blur are detected on your phone. Memory does not upload your photos for this. Object recognition uses Google ML Kit's on-device image labelling; images are processed on your phone. Google's ML Kit terms describe any technical or diagnostic data the library itself may send, which does not include your photos.

You can switch analysis off in Settings, or use Local-only mode.

When Memory uses the internet

Memory works fully offline. It connects to the internet only in these cases:

1) Update check and download. Memory reads a small public information file from Firebase Realtime Database (Google) and/or the GitHub releases API, and downloads new versions from GitHub. Like any web request, these services can see your IP address and basic technical details. Memory sends no personal data and no photos. You can turn off automatic checks in Settings.

2) Place names. To show city names, Memory asks Android's built-in geocoder for the place at a photo's coordinates. Depending on your phone, this system service may send those coordinates to its provider (usually Google) over the network. You can turn this off in Settings ('Look up place names') or with Local-only mode.

3) Backup and shared albums. Only if you choose a folder, Memory copies photos into that folder using Android's storage picker. If the folder belongs to a cloud app (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox and so on), that provider's terms and privacy policy apply. We do not run any server and cannot see these files.

Local-only mode in Settings turns off all three.

What we do not do

We do not collect your photos, videos, location or personal information. We do not require an account. We show no ads and use no analytics or tracking tools of our own. We do not sell or share your data.

Sharing

When you share a photo, Memory hands only the files you picked to the app you chose. You can remove location data from shared photos, or share a smaller resized copy, which never includes location.

Permissions

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (older Android): to show your photos and videos.

READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED: lets you give access to selected photos only (Android 14+).

ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: to read the location saved in your photos for place grouping and maps.

USE_BIOMETRIC: to protect the Hidden album and App Lock with Android's prompt.

INTERNET: for update checks, and place-name lookup (see above).

REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES: only to install a Memory update you chose to download. Not part of the Google Play version.

Your choices and deleting your data

Everything Memory stores is on your device. You can clear it from Android Settings > Apps > Memory > Storage, or by uninstalling. Deleted photos go to Android's Recently Deleted for about 30 days. Copies you made with Backup or shared albums stay in the folder you chose until you delete them there.

Children

Memory is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Security

Memory uses Android's app sandbox, requires authentication for the Hidden album and App Lock, and downloads updates over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked and updated.

Changes to this policy

If Memory's data practices change, this page and the in-app copy will be updated, and the date below will change.
